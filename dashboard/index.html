DRCF: two brokers, no single point of trust

A replay of one real run of the DRCF pipeline. Both brokers ran as separate C++ processes: ASCON-128 inside garbled circuits (EMP), CKKS cross terms (SEAL 4.1.2), 2-party threshold ECDSA following Doerner et al. (S&P 2024) and 2-party Schnorr on secp256k1. Every share, ciphertext, timing and byte count below comes from that run.
