DRCF: two brokers, no single point of trust

A replay of one real run of the DRCF pipeline. Both brokers ran as separate C++ processes: ASCON-128 inside garbled circuits (EMP), CKKS cross terms (SEAL 4.1.2), 2-party threshold ECDSA following Doerner et al. (S&P 2024) and 2-party Schnorr on secp256k1. Every share, ciphertext, timing and byte count below comes from that run.

    Try the Table A1 protocols on your data

    drcf.py

    The six IoT protocols of Table A1, each with the model of the paper it cites. Run any of them on your own CSV:

    IAnomaly detection

    PARX (Ardakanian et al. 2014). One OLS model per hour and day type, previous 3 days as lags, temperature terms; flag if |y − ŷ| > 3σ. CKKS + garbled-circuit decision.

    python3 drcf.py --protocol 1 --data meter.csv --time ts --value kwh --temp temperature
    IIAnomaly detection

    Prophet (Jaiswal et al. 2021). Trend + daily, yearly, weekday/weekend seasonality fitted on each share; 99% interval; zero readings flagged.

    python3 drcf.py --protocol 2 --data meter.csv --time ts --value kwh
    IIILoad forecasting

    SWHSA (Alberg & Last 2018). SARIMA(0,0,1)(0,1,1)24 on a 96-day sliding window, 24 h ahead, drift-triggered refit. Linear predictors in CKKS.

    python3 drcf.py --protocol 3 --data load.csv --time ts --value kw
    IVLoad forecasting

    RBF SVR (Chen, Chang & Lin 2004). Daily peak, 31 days ahead recursively; 7 previous peaks + calendar bits. Peaks and SVR in a garbled circuit.

    python3 drcf.py --protocol 4 --data load.csv --time ts --value kw --series client
    VPrice forecasting

    ARIMA (Contreras et al. 2003). Multiplicative ARIMA on log prices (Eq. 3), 24 h ahead each day. Linear predictors in CKKS.

    python3 drcf.py --protocol 5 --data price.csv --time ts --value price
    VIPrice forecasting

    SVRARIMA (Che & Wang 2010). RBF SVR on lags 1–3 in a garbled circuit, ARIMA on its residuals in CKKS, one hour ahead.

    python3 drcf.py --protocol 6 --data price.csv --time ts --value price
